Executive Proposal

Product Proposal: Non-MDM Workplace Attendance & App Restriction Architecture

Document Version: 1.0 • Audience: Product Supervisor, Engineering Lead, HR / Compliance Team
Core Theme: Personal Devices (BYOD) Workforce Productivity & Privacy-Preserving App Restriction

1. Executive Summary

Traditional workforce productivity tools face a severe dilemma when deployed on personal mobile devices (BYOD):

  • Mobile Device Management (MDM) solutions (such as Microsoft Intune or VMware Workspace ONE) require invasive device administrator privileges, remote wipe authority, and device-level monitoring. Employees rightfully resist installing them on personal hardware due to privacy concerns.
  • Accessibility-based blockers request permissions that can inspect personal banking, private messaging, and screen contents, triggering Play Store rejections, OS security warnings, and legal liabilities.

This document proposes a lightweight, privacy-first alternative: an attendance-based app restriction engine that:

  1. Operates strictly while the employee is timed in.
  2. Requires zero MDM / zero device administrator rights.
  3. Uses no Accessibility Service (preventing any access to screen contents, keystrokes, or private messages).
  4. Configures restrictions dynamically from the company backend based on employee login codes and roles.
  5. Disables all restrictions the instant the shift ends or the employee clocks out.

2. Why MDM Fails for Personal Devices (BYOD)

If your product leadership has concerns about MDM, their concerns are backed by industry reality:

MDM Risk Factor Impact on BYOD Workforce
"Nuclear Option" Remote Wipe MDM profiles give IT departments authority to remotely erase the entire phone. Employees fear losing personal family photos, WhatsApp chats, and personal banking apps.
High Licensing Cost Enterprise MDMs cost $4 to $10 per employee per month in recurring subscription fees.
Certificate / Traffic Inspection MDMs can route traffic through enterprise proxies, theoretically decrypting private web traffic.
Legal & Labor Law Violations In many jurisdictions (such as California Labor Code, EU/GDPR, and the Philippines Labor Code), enforcing off-hours device restrictions or monitoring on personal assets exposes companies to labor lawsuits.

3. The Solution: App-Level Enforcement (No MDM, No Accessibility)

Instead of attempting to control the entire operating system via MDM, the app relies strictly on two standard, user-transparent Android system APIs:

1. UsageStatsManager (Usage Access)

Allows the attendance service to query which package is currently in the foreground every 900ms. It has zero access to screen pixels, text fields, keystrokes, camera, or personal app content. It only evaluates: "Is the frontmost window TikTok or Slack?"

2. SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Display Over Other Apps)

When a restricted package is detected during an active shift, a friendly translucent overlay appears displaying: "This app is restricted during your attendance session. Please return to work tasks or clock out." Tapping "Go Back" smoothly redirects the employee to their home screen or the company attendance portal.

4. End-to-End System Flow

[Step 1: Authentication] Employee Phone ──> Enters Login Code ("EMP-4821") ──> Company Backend Backend ──> Validates Role & Department ──> Returns JWT + Profile [Step 2: Clock-In & Policy Delivery] Employee Phone ──> Taps "CLOCK IN" ──> Company Backend Backend ──> Logs Attendance Timestamp ──> Sends Policy JSON (Blocklist/Allowlist) [Step 3: Dynamic Local Enforcement (During Shift Only)] App Service ──> Polls UsageStatsManager (every 900ms) ├─ Blocked App (TikTok, Games) ──> Shows Translucent Lock Overlay └─ Work App / Home Launcher ──> Allowed Uninterrupted [Step 4: Clock-Out & Instant Release] Employee Phone ──> Taps "CLOCK OUT" (or Shift Ends) ──> Company Backend App Service ──> Kills Background Monitor & Disarms Overlay Phone State ──> 100% unrestricted personal phone again

Why This Architecture Wins:

  • Zero App Updates Required: When a new viral game or app emerges, HR or Operations adds the package name to the backend portal. All employee devices immediately adopt the new rule on their next shift.
  • Department Tailoring:
    • Field Technicians: Can use Google Maps and Camera; games and social media blocked.
    • Social Media Coordinators: Can use Instagram and TikTok; games and personal browsers blocked.
    • Call Center / Retail Staff: Strict distraction-free profile.

6. Anti-Lockout Fail-Safes (Safety Guarantees)

A primary concern for product leadership is ensuring employees are never locked out of vital functions on their personal phones. The system enforces five critical safety guarantees:

  1. Emergency & Dialer Immunity: The phone dialer (Intent.ACTION_DIAL) and emergency services (911, 112, 999) can never be blocked under any circumstances.
  2. Device Settings Immunity: com.android.settings is strictly whitelisted. Employees can always adjust WiFi, Bluetooth, or manage their device settings.
  3. Home Launcher Immunity: Any app declaring Intent.CATEGORY_HOME (system launcher) is protected, ensuring phone navigation never freezes.
  4. Shift Dead-Man's Switch (Auto-Timeout): If an employee leaves work and forgets to clock out, the service automatically deactivates after a configurable threshold (e.g., 8.5 hours).
  5. Direct Escape Hatch on Lock Screen: The lock overlay features an immediate "Go to Attendance" button allowing the user to clock out or request a break at any moment.

8. Summary & Recommendation for Product Leadership

Key Takeaway for Management:
This proposed architecture achieves 100% of the operational objective (preventing TikTok, Instagram, gaming, and web surfing while on company time) with 0% of the legal, financial, and cultural baggage of MDM.

  • Employees accept it because it is completely transparent, does not inspect private texts or bank accounts, and completely deactivates when their shift ends.
  • Operations gains full flexibility through dynamic, role-based restriction policies delivered from your existing backend database.
  • The company avoids recurring enterprise MDM license fees while retaining full intellectual property ownership of the attendance solution.
